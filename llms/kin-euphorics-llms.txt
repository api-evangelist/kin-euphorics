# Kin Euphorics

> Kin Euphorics is a non-alcoholic functional beverage company making "euphorics" — botanical drinks blending adaptogens, nootropics, and botanics designed to lift mood and offer a social alternative to alcohol. The brand sells direct to consumers through a Shopify storefront that exposes a modern agent-commerce surface (UCP/MCP shopping) and Shopify customer-account OpenID Connect.

This llms.txt was generated by the API Evangelist enrichment pipeline from the storefront's public discovery surface (robots.txt, /.well-known/*). Product catalog and marketing pages are served behind a Cloudflare challenge; agents should transact via the UCP/MCP endpoint below, and checkout requires explicit human approval.
